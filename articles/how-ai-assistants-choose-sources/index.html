How AI assistants choose which websites to cite: what the research says

By SoftScout. Published 24 November 2026.

In short

AI assistants that search the web pick pages they can reach, understand quickly and trust. Research found that adding sources, statistics and quotations made content up to about 40% more visible in AI answers, while keyword stuffing made it worse. Which sites get cited also differs a lot between ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features. First make sure AI crawlers are not blocked, then write clear pages that answer one question well.

How an AI answer finds its sources

When an assistant answers a question about something current, it usually searches the web, reads a handful of pages and writes a summary, often with links to the pages it used. Your page can only be one of those links if three things are true: the assistant's crawler can reach it, the page plainly answers the question, and it looks more reliable than the other candidates.

What the research found

A study by researchers from Princeton and other institutions, published at the KDD 2024 conference, tested ways of rewriting web content and measured how visible it became inside AI-generated answers. Their headline result: optimisation "can boost visibility by up to 40%" (Aggarwal et al.). On their benchmark, compared with unchanged text:

Change to the textEffect on visibility (word-count measure)
Adding relevant quotationsabout +41%
Adding statisticsabout +31%
Citing sourcesabout +28%
Improving fluency (clearer, smoother writing)about +28%
Keyword stuffingabout −8% (worse)

Percentages calculated from the paper's Table 1 against its no-change baseline. These are results on a research benchmark, not a guarantee for any single site, and the authors note that results vary by subject area.

Each assistant cites different sources

A Q1 2026 study by Tinuiti and Profound, reported by Search Engine Land, tracked commercial questions across seven AI platforms and concluded there is "no universal top source." For example, Reddit made up 24% of Perplexity's citations in January 2026, more than 5% on ChatGPT, but only 0.1% on Google Gemini. The same report cites BrightEdge data that only about 17% of Google AI Overview citations overlap with page-one search results. In other words, ranking well in normal search helps, but it is not the whole story, and you should check each assistant separately.

Make sure AI crawlers can reach your site

Some hosting and security services can block AI crawlers. Cloudflare, for example, now lets site owners set separate rules for search, agent and training bots; for domains added from 15 September 2026, training and agent bots are blocked by default on pages that show ads, while search bots stay allowed. If you want to be cited, check that the search and answer crawlers you care about are allowed in your hosting settings and in robots.txt.

Practical steps

  • Answer one question per page, and give the short answer in the first paragraph.
  • Back claims with numbers and named sources, and link to them.
  • Use plain headings, lists and tables so each part makes sense on its own.
  • Show who wrote it and when, and update the date when you revise it.
  • Add structured data (for example Article and FAQ markup) so the page type is clear to machines.
  • Be consistent everywhere: the same company name, products and facts on your site, profiles and listings.
  • Earn mentions on other sites that the assistants already cite in your field, such as industry directories, reviews and genuine community discussions.
  • Check results by asking the assistants the questions your customers ask, and record which sites they cite. Ask several times: answers change from run to run.

What not to expect

No one can guarantee that an assistant will cite a page. Answers vary between runs, between assistants and over time, and a new site with few links from elsewhere usually takes longer to be picked up. Treat AI visibility as a result of being genuinely useful and easy to read, not as a trick.

Questions and answers

Does keyword stuffing help AI assistants find my page?

No. In the KDD 2024 GEO study, keyword stuffing reduced visibility in AI answers by about 8% compared with unchanged text. Clear writing with sources and statistics performed far better.

Do I need to rank on Google to be cited by AI?

It helps, but it isn't enough on its own. Data reported by Search Engine Land suggests only about 17% of Google AI Overview citations overlap with page-one results, and each AI platform favours different sources.

How can I check if AI assistants mention my site?

Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features the questions your customers would ask, several times each, and note which sites they cite. Dedicated tracking tools can do this at scale.
