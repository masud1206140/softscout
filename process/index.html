How we work

Clear steps, written scope and evidence for every finding.

  1. Brief. You tell us your website, your goal and your deadline. A short email is enough.
  2. Scope. We reply with the pages or tasks we will cover, what you will receive, the delivery date and the price. Work starts when you agree.
  3. Work. We test or review your live site and record evidence: screenshots, steps and page addresses.
  4. Report. You get the report in a spreadsheet or document, with the most serious problems first.
  5. Re-check. When you have made fixes, we check those items again and update the report.

What we need from you

  • Your website or app address
  • What matters most: a page, a flow, a product or a campaign
  • A test account, if parts of the site need a log-in
  • One person we can ask questions

How we handle your data

  • We use test accounts and test data, not real customer details.
  • We don't share your reports or findings with anyone else.
  • We don't show your work as an example without your written permission.
  • When the job ends, you can remove our access.
