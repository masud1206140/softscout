Services

Pick one service or combine them. Every job starts with a written scope, so you know what you will get before we begin.

Website and app testing

We use your site the way a new customer would and write down everything that gets in their way.

What we check

  • Links, buttons, forms, sign-up, log-in and checkout steps
  • Phones, tablets and computers, in the common browsers
  • Whether the text, prices and product details are correct
  • Whether help pages and guides match what is actually on screen
  • Basic speed and accessibility problems that stop people using the page

What you get

An issue log (spreadsheet) with each problem's page, how serious it is, what we saw, the steps to repeat it, a screenshot, and a suggested fix. After you fix things, we check again.

Customer research

We work out who is most likely to buy from you, so your marketing talks to the right people.

  • A short profile of your best-fit customers: industry, size, region
  • The job titles that decide and the ones that use the product
  • Signs that a company is ready to buy now
  • A starting list of target companies, if you need one

Content updates

Websites go out of date quietly. We find what's wrong and fix the wording.

  • A page-by-page review of outdated claims, wrong names, broken promises and gaps
  • Rewrites in plain language that a first-time visitor understands
  • Image descriptions and page details that help search engines and AI assistants read your site correctly
  • Regular updates for news and blog pages, if you want them

Content for ads

An ad works when the ad, its wording and the page it opens all match what the person was looking for.

  • Several versions of ad wording to test against each other
  • Ideas for the search words and topics to target
  • A check that the landing page answers the question the ad raised
  • A simple plan for testing and reading the results

This works for search ads, social media ads and the new ad formats inside AI assistants.
